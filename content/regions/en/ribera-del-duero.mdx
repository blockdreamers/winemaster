## At a glance

Ribera del Duero lies north of Madrid in Castilla y León, on a **plateau at roughly 700–1,000 m**. Summer days are scorching and nights cool, winters are cold, and spring frosts are frequent: an **extreme continental** climate.

## Tinto Fino

Here Tempranillo is called **Tinto Fino** or **Tinta del País**. The big day-night swing lets the grapes ripen by day and keep their acid at night, producing deep-coloured reds with firm tannins and lively acidity. The rules allow small amounts of Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Malbec and others to be blended in.

## Reputation

**Vega Sicilia**, one of Spain's most celebrated wines, is here, and the region uses the same Crianza, Reserva and Gran Reserva ageing terms as Rioja. Chapter 23 goes into more detail.
