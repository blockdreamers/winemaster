## At a glance

Tuscany is in central Italy, facing the Tyrrhenian Sea. It has a warm **Mediterranean** climate, but inland hills cool the nights and preserve acidity. The star is **Sangiovese**, Italy's most planted red grape.

## Main wines

| Wine | Variety | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Chianti DOCG | Mainly Sangiovese | From light and zesty to rich |
| Chianti Classico DOCG | At least 80% Sangiovese | The original Chianti area. Black rooster logo |
| Brunello di Montalcino DOCG | 100% Sangiovese | Rich and age-worthy. Released about 5 years after harvest |
| Vino Nobile di Montepulciano DOCG | Mainly Sangiovese | Between Chianti and Brunello in style |
| Bolgheri DOC | Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Cabernet Franc | Bordeaux-style blends from the coast |

## Super Tuscans

From the mid-20th century, some producers stepped outside the rules of the time, using international varieties such as Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot or ageing wine in new French-style barrels. At first these were sold as table wine, but as wines like Sassicaia and Tignanello won great fame, they came to be called **Super Tuscans**. Today they are mostly sold as **IGT Toscana** or Bolgheri DOC. Chapter 22 goes into more detail.
