## At a glance

Veneto runs from the southern foothills of the Alps to the Adriatic Sea in north-east Italy. The Alps and Lake Garda moderate the climate, making it a relatively mild **continental** one, and it is among Italy's largest wine-producing regions.

## Main wines

| Wine | Variety | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Valpolicella | Mainly Corvina, with Rondinella and others | Light, zesty red with cherry aromas |
| Valpolicella Ripasso | Same as above | Red made richer by refermenting on Amarone skins |
| Amarone della Valpolicella DOCG | Same as above (dried grapes) | High-alcohol, rich dry red |
| Recioto della Valpolicella DOCG | Same as above (dried grapes) | Sweet red |
| Soave | Garganega | Dry white with almond and lemon notes |
| Prosecco | Glera | Light, fruity tank-method sparkling wine |

## Appassimento

The technique of **drying** harvested grapes **for several months** in a well-ventilated place is called **appassimento**. As water evaporates, sugar and flavour concentrate. Ferment these grapes dry and you get Amarone; leave sweetness and you get Recioto. How Prosecco is made is covered in detail in Chapter 29.
