## At a glance

The Loire Valley follows the Loire river for hundreds of kilometres, from its Atlantic mouth near Nantes to Sancerre inland. The west has a cool **maritime** climate shaped by the ocean, becoming more **continental** further east. It is cool overall, so its wines are known for high acidity.

## From west to east

| Area | Main varieties | Key appellations | Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nantais | Melon de Bourgogne | Muscadet | Light, salty dry whites aged sur lie |
| Anjou-Saumur | Chenin Blanc, Cabernet Franc | Savennières, Coteaux du Layon, Saumur-Champigny | Dry and sweet Chenin, light reds |
| Touraine | Chenin Blanc, Cabernet Franc | Vouvray, Chinon, Bourgueil | Chenin at every sweetness level, herbal reds |
| Centre | Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Noir | Sancerre, Pouilly-Fumé | Zesty, mineral Sauvignon Blanc |

## The many faces of Chenin Blanc

Loire **Chenin Blanc** has such high acidity that it can be made in balance as dry, off-dry, noble-rot sweet or sparkling wine. That's why, even under the same Vouvray label, you need to check the sweetness term (sec, demi-sec, moelleux). Chapters 15 and 21 go into more detail.
