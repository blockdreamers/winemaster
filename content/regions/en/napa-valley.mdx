## At a glance

Napa Valley is a narrow valley about 50 km long, a little over an hour north of San Francisco. Days are warm with strong sun, but fog and wind rolling in from San Pablo Bay to the south lower night temperatures, helping the grapes keep their acidity.

## Napa's face: Cabernet Sauvignon

Napa Cabernet Sauvignon is known for ripe blackcurrant and black cherry, deep colour, smooth yet firm tannins, and vanilla from new oak barrels. With more sun than Bordeaux, the fruit is more generous and the alcohol tends to be higher.

## The 1976 Judgment of Paris

At a blind tasting in Paris in 1976, Napa's Chateau Montelena (Chardonnay) and Stag's Leap Wine Cellars (Cabernet Sauvignon) took first place over famous Burgundies and Bordeaux. The event shattered the idea that great wine only came from France. Chapter 3 tells the story in detail.
