## At a glance

Piedmont means "foot of the mountains", and the region in north-west Italy is ringed by the Alps and the Apennines. It has a **continental** climate with cold winters and hot summers, and in autumn thick fog settles over the hills of the **Langhe** and **Roero** around Alba and around Asti.

## Main wines

| Wine | Variety | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Barolo DOCG | Nebbiolo | High tannin and acidity, rose and tar. At least 38 months of ageing (18+ in wood) |
| Barbaresco DOCG | Nebbiolo | A little softer. At least 26 months of ageing (9+ in wood) |
| Barbera d'Asti / d'Alba | Barbera | Fruit-forward red with high acidity and low tannin |
| Dolcetto d'Alba | Dolcetto | Soft, light red |
| Moscato d'Asti DOCG | Moscato Bianco | Sweet, lightly sparkling white around 5% alcohol |
| Gavi DOCG | Cortese | Zesty dry white |

## A hierarchy of slopes

Late-ripening **Nebbiolo** takes the best spots on sunny, south-facing slopes, while Barbera and Dolcetto are planted in less favoured sites. In Barolo and Barbaresco, labels increasingly name single vineyards (MGA, or menzioni) as in Burgundy. Chapter 22 goes into more detail.
