## At a glance

Stellenbosch is a university town about 50 km east of Cape Town and the centre of South African wine. Grapes have been grown here since the late 17th century, and the climate is Mediterranean with hot, dry summers. Sea breezes from False Bay to the south and the south-easterly wind called the "Cape Doctor" cool the heat, and the height and aspect of the mountain slopes create a wide range of climates.

## The birthplace of Pinotage

In 1925 Professor Perold of Stellenbosch University created Pinotage by crossing **Pinot Noir** with **Cinsault**. South Africans called Cinsault "Hermitage" at the time, so the two names were combined into the name we know today. Red blends including Pinotage, called "Cape blends", are also made here. The university is still the centre of South African viticulture and winemaking research. Chapters 18 and 27 go into more detail.
