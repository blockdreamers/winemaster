## At a glance

The Douro is the valley where Spain's Duero river crosses the border and continues into Portugal. Hand-built terraces cover the steep **schist** slopes on both banks, and mountains to the west block Atlantic rain, so summers are very hot and dry. It is counted among the oldest demarcated wine regions in the world.

## Varieties

| Variety | Role |
|---|---|
| Touriga Nacional | Deep colour, floral and black-fruit aromas, firm structure |
| Touriga Franca | Aromatic and the most widely planted |
| Tinta Roriz (= Tempranillo) | Red fruit and softness |
| Tinta Barroca, Tinto Cão | Add colour, acidity and ageing potential |

## Port and table wine

**Port** is a fortified wine made by adding grape spirit to red wine partway through fermentation, stopping it and keeping the sweetness. Traditionally it was shipped downriver to **Vila Nova de Gaia** at the river mouth to age. Recently, **dry reds** from the same varieties have also earned high praise. Port styles (Ruby, Tawny, Vintage and more) are covered in Chapter 29.
