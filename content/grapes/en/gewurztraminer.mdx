## Meet the grape

"Gewürz" means spice in German. The skins are pinkish, and the wine can be a deep gold. Its aromas are so strong that it's one of the easiest grapes to identify in a tasting.

## How it tastes

- **Aromas**: **lychee**, **rose**, spices like ginger and cinnamon, and tropical fruit.
- **Structure**: **low acidity**, full body, high alcohol. A slightly oily texture.
- **Sweetness**: from dry to off-dry, late harvest (Vendanges Tardives) and noble-rot sweet.

Because acidity is low, harvesting late can make it flabby, while harvesting early leaves the aromas underdeveloped. Picking time matters a lot for this grape.

## Where it grows

| Region | Style |
|---|---|
| Alsace (France) | The most famous. Opulent and intensely aromatic, dry to sweet |
| Alto Adige (Italy) | A slightly fresher style |
| Germany, New Zealand, USA | Various sweetness levels |

## With food

It pairs well with heavily spiced food: Indian curry, Thai food, Chinese dishes, and pungent Munster cheese. It's also worth trying with spicy Korean stir-fries.
