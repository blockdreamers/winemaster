## Meet the grape

Cabernet Franc is **a parent of Cabernet Sauvignon** (the other is Sauvignon Blanc). It **ripens earlier and has thinner skins** than Cabernet Sauvignon, so it ripens well even in cool places. That's why it can make red wine on its own in northern regions like the Loire.

## How it tastes

- **Aromas**: raspberry, red cherry and violet. When underripe, **green notes** like bell pepper or leaves stand out.
- **Structure**: medium body and tannin with refreshing acidity. Lighter and softer than Cabernet Sauvignon.
- **Ageing**: wines from good years gain pencil shavings and earthy notes and last a long time.

## Where it grows

| Region | Style |
|---|---|
| Loire (Chinon, Bourgueil, Saumur-Champigny) | Light, fragrant single-variety reds |
| Bordeaux Right Bank (Saint-Émilion) | Blended with Merlot, adding aroma and structure |
| Bordeaux Left Bank | A minor supporting role in blends |

## With food

With refreshing acidity and moderate tannin, it goes well with roast pork, chicken and herb-driven dishes. Served slightly chilled, it's lovely in summer too.
