## Meet the grape

Nebbiolo buds early and **ripens very late**. A widely told story says its name comes from the fog (Italian *nebbia*) that blankets the hills at autumn harvest. It is sensitive to climate and soil, so it's hard to make good wine from it outside Piedmont.

## How it tastes

- **Colour**: surprisingly **pale**. Even young wines can show orange at the rim.
- **Aromas**: rose, red cherry, tar, liquorice. With age, dried fruit, leather and truffle.
- **Structure**: unlike its colour, **both acidity and tannin are high**, with ample alcohol and body.

> If you take it for a light wine from its pale colour, the first sip will startle you.

## Signature wines

| Wine | Character |
|---|---|
| Barolo | At least 38 months of ageing (18 in wood). Powerful and long-lived |
| Barbaresco | A little softer than Barolo and drinkable a bit sooner |
| Langhe Nebbiolo | Lighter, for drinking earlier |

## With food

Strong tannin and acidity call for rich, protein-heavy food. It goes well with mushroom risotto, truffle dishes, braised short ribs (galbi-jjim) and aged cheese.
