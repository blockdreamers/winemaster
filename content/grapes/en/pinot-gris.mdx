## Meet the grape

Pinot Gris is a mutation of Pinot Noir with **pinkish-grey** skins. It's famous for being one grape whose style changes with its name. The Italian name **Pinot Grigio** usually signals a light, zesty style, while the French name **Pinot Gris** suggests an opulent one.

## Two styles

| | Pinot Grigio | Pinot Gris (Alsace) |
|---|---|---|
| Harvest | Early | Fully ripe |
| Aromas | Lemon, green apple, pear. Subtle | Peach, pear, honey, ginger |
| Structure | Light body, zesty acidity | Full body, medium acidity, high alcohol |
| Sweetness | Dry | Dry to sweet |

## Where it grows

| Region | Style |
|---|---|
| Veneto, Friuli, Trentino-Alto Adige (Italy) | Light, zesty Pinot Grigio |
| Alsace (France) | Opulent and spicy. Late-harvest sweet wines too |
| Willamette Valley (Oregon) | A fruity middle style |
| New Zealand, Germany (Grauburgunder) | From dry to off-dry |

## With food

Pinot Grigio suits seafood pasta, salads and light fish dishes, while Alsace Pinot Gris goes well with pork, mushroom dishes, smoked salmon, and sweet-savoury Korean dishes like braised short ribs.
