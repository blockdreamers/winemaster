## Meet the grape

Syrah is a grape with **deep colour and firm tannins**. Its home is France's **Northern Rhône** (Hermitage, Côte-Rôtie and others), and in Australia, under the name **Shiraz**, it became the most important red grape. The same grape changes its face dramatically with climate.

## How it tastes

- **Aromas**: black fruit like blackberry and plum, with **black pepper** and violet. With age, smoked meat, leather and earth.
- **Structure**: deep colour, medium-plus to high tannin, full body.

## Cool Syrah, warm Shiraz

| | Cool climate (Northern Rhône) | Warm climate (Barossa Valley) |
|---|---|---|
| Fruit | Blackberry, fresh black fruit | Ripe blackberry, jam, chocolate |
| Spice | Black pepper, violet | Liquorice, sweet spice |
| Structure | Medium alcohol, refreshing acidity | High alcohol, full body |

In the Northern Rhône it is sometimes co-fermented with a little of the white grape Viognier, and in the Southern Rhône and Australia it is often blended with Grenache and Mourvèdre (GSM blends).

## With food

Its peppery notes and full body suit grilled and marinated meat such as lamb, galbi (Korean short ribs) and barbecue.
