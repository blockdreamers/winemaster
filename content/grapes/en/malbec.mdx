## Meet the grape

Malbec is a grape of **Cahors** in South-West France, where it is also called **Côt**. It was once used in Bordeaux blends, but little of it remains there now. Today Malbec is synonymous with **Mendoza, Argentina**. The high altitude of the Andean foothills brings strong sun and cool nights, giving deep colour, ripe fruit and freshness together.

## How it tastes

- **Colour**: very deep purple.
- **Aromas**: blackberry, plum, blueberry, violet. Cocoa and vanilla with oak ageing.
- **Structure**: medium-plus tannin but with a **soft** texture, and generous body.

## Two homes compared

| | Cahors (France) | Mendoza (Argentina) |
|---|---|---|
| Style | Firm tannins, earthy | Round, rich fruit, soft tannins |
| Aromas | Black fruit, earth | Blackberry, plum, violet |

## With food

It's famous as a partner for Argentine grilled beef (asado). With Korean food, it goes well with grilled beef short ribs or thinly sliced brisket.
