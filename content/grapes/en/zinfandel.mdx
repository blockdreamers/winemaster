## Meet the grape

Zinfandel was long considered "California's grape", but DNA analysis revealed it to be the same variety as **Tribidrag from Croatia** and **Primitivo** from Puglia in southern Italy. Its berries **ripen unevenly** within the bunch, so underripe berries and raisin-like shrivelled ones can end up in the same bunch.

## How it tastes

- **Aromas**: blackberry jam, blueberry, raisin, liquorice, sweet spice.
- **Structure**: **high alcohol** and full body, with medium acidity and tannin.
- **Old vines**: California has vines nearly 100 years old, which give low yields but rich wines.

## Many faces

| Style | Character |
|---|---|
| Red Zinfandel (Sonoma and others) | Jammy fruit, high alcohol, full body |
| White Zinfandel | A pale pink, sweet rosé. Light and low in alcohol |
| Primitivo (Puglia) | A warm southern Italian style with ripe black fruit |

## With food

Its rich, sweet fruit goes well with sweet sauces and marinades, such as barbecue sauce, pork ribs and Korean sweet-and-spicy fried chicken.
