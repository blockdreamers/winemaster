## Meet the grape

Sauvignon Blanc is an **aromatic** grape with very strong varietal aromas. To preserve them, it is mostly **fermented cool without oak** and made to be drunk fresh, within 1–3 years of production. Together with Cabernet Franc, it is also a parent of Cabernet Sauvignon.

## Two families of aromas

| Family | Aromas | When they get stronger |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Fresh-cut grass, bell pepper, asparagus, gooseberry | Cool or shaded grapes |
| Tropical | Passion fruit, grapefruit, guava | Grapes that got plenty of sun |

## Where it grows

| Region | Style |
|---|---|
| Sancerre, Pouilly-Fumé (Loire) | Restrained fruit, high acidity, flinty minerality |
| Marlborough (New Zealand) | An intense style bursting with passion fruit and grassy notes |
| Bordeaux | Blended with Semillon. Oak-aged whites from Pessac-Léognan, noble-rot Sauternes |
| Chile, South Africa | Zesty styles shaped by cool coastal breezes |

In California, an oaked style is sometimes called **Fumé Blanc**.

## With food

High acidity and herbal notes suit fresh vegetables, herbs and tangy food. Goat's cheese with Sancerre is a classic pairing, and it's also good with seafood salads and seafood dishes with minari (water dropwort).
