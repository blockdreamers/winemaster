## Meet the grape

Grüner Veltliner is Austria's signature grape and covers the largest area of the country's vineyards. Its range is wide, from easy-drinking everyday wines to concentrated wines that can age for a long time.

## How it tastes

- **Aromas**: green apple, lemon, grapefruit, herbs, and the grape's hallmark, **white pepper**.
- **Structure**: high acidity, light to full body. Mostly dry.
- **Winemaking**: mostly made fresh without oak flavour; top wines sometimes gain texture from large old casks or lees ageing.

## Where it grows

| Region | Style |
|---|---|
| Wachau | Steep terraces along the Danube. Concentrated, powerful dry wines |
| Kamptal, Kremstal | Quality similar to the Wachau, in a range of styles |
| Weinviertel | Fresh, with vivid pepper |

## With food

High acidity and peppery notes go well with vegetable and herb dishes. Austrian schnitzel is the classic match, and it's also an easy partner for tonkatsu, Korean namul side dishes and vegetable pancakes.
