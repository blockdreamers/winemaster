## Meet the grape

Cabernet Sauvignon was born in 17th-century Bordeaux from a chance cross between **Cabernet Franc** and **Sauvignon Blanc**. Its thick skins and small berries give deep colour and **strong tannins**. It ripens late, so it needs a warm enough site.

## How it tastes

- **Aromas**: blackcurrant (cassis) and black cherry. When underripe, it shows grassy notes like bell pepper.
- **Structure**: high tannin and high acidity. Astringent when young, it softens with age.
- **A match for oak**: ageing in new oak adds vanilla, cedar and toast.

## Where it grows

| Region | Style |
|---|---|
| Bordeaux Left Bank | Blended with Merlot and others. Cedar, graphite, elegant acidity |
| Napa Valley | Mostly single-variety. Generous fruit, high alcohol |
| Coonawarra (Australia) | Mint, eucalyptus |
| Maipo Valley (Chile) | Blackcurrant and mint, good value |

## With food

Tannic wines soften when they meet **protein and fat**. Charcoal-grilled beef, lamb chops and aged cheese are the classics.
