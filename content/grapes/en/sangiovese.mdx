## Meet the grape

Sangiovese is one of Italy's most planted red grapes and the star of **Tuscany**. It ripens fairly late, so the best wines come from sunny slopes. It goes by different names in different places: **Brunello** in Montalcino and **Prugnolo Gentile** in Montepulciano.

## How it tastes

- **Aromas**: sour cherry, red plum, tomato leaf, dried herbs. With age, black tea, leather and earth.
- **Structure**: **high acidity**, medium-plus to high tannin, medium body.
- **Colour**: medium ruby that turns garnet early.

## Signature wines

| Wine | Style |
|---|---|
| Chianti Classico | Mainly Sangiovese. Sour cherry, herbs, refreshing acidity |
| Brunello di Montalcino | 100% Sangiovese. Richer and longer-ageing |
| Vino Nobile di Montepulciano | Mainly Prugnolo Gentile |
| Super Tuscans | Sometimes blended with Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot |

## With food

Its high acidity matches the tang of tomatoes, so it pairs well with tomato-sauce pasta, pizza and grilled meat. With Korean food, tangy meat dishes like braised pork with kimchi work too.
