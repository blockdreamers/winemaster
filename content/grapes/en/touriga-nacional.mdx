## Meet the grape

Touriga Nacional is a native grape grown in the **Douro** and **Dão** in northern Portugal. Small berries and thick skins mean low yields, but the juice is **very deep in colour, tannin and aroma**. Of the many grapes used for Port, it is the most highly regarded.

## How it tastes

- **Aromas**: black fruit like blackberry and blueberry, with floral notes like **violet**. Bergamot and liquorice are sometimes mentioned too.
- **Structure**: deep colour, strong tannin, full body. Acidity stays moderate.

## Port and dry wine

| Style | Role |
|---|---|
| Port | Blended with Touriga Franca, Tinta Roriz and others. The backbone of colour, tannin and floral aroma |
| Douro dry red | On its own or blended. Dense reds with great ageing potential |
| Dão | Elegant reds from a slightly cooler region |

Port is a fortified wine in which spirit is added during fermentation to keep the sweetness. Touriga Nacional's deep colour and tannin build plenty of structure even with a short maceration, and give Vintage Port the strength to last for decades (Chapter 29).

## With food

Dry reds go well with lamb and beef stew, and Port with blue cheese and dark chocolate.
