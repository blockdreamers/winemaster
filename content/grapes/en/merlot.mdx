## Meet the grape

Merlot **ripens earlier** than Cabernet Sauvignon, and its large berries and thin skins give **soft tannins**. It ripens well even on cool, water-retentive clay soils, so it is the most planted grape on Bordeaux's Right Bank (Saint-Émilion, Pomerol).

## How it tastes

- **Aromas**: plum, blackberry, cherry. With age, chocolate and fruitcake.
- **Structure**: medium acidity and tannin with generous body. Even first-time wine drinkers find it easy to enjoy.
- **By climate**: red fruit and herbal notes in cool places; jammy black fruit in warm ones.

## Its relationship with Cabernet Sauvignon

In a Bordeaux blend, if Cabernet Sauvignon is the **skeleton**, Merlot is the **flesh**, filling the gaps between firm tannins with soft fruit. So the Left Bank blends Cabernet first plus Merlot, while the Right Bank blends Merlot first plus Cabernet Franc.
