## Meet the grape

The name Tempranillo comes from the Spanish *temprano*, "early", because it **ripens earlier** than other grapes. Its thick skins give fairly deep colour, and with moderate acidity, the best wines come from cool high ground or places with big day-night temperature swings. In Portugal it is called **Tinta Roriz** and is used in Port too.

## How it tastes

- **Aromas**: red cherry, strawberry, plum. From oak ageing, vanilla, coconut (especially American oak) and clove.
- **With age**: tertiary notes like leather, tobacco leaf and dried fig.
- **Structure**: medium acidity, medium-plus tannin and body.

## Where it grows

| Region | Style |
|---|---|
| Rioja | Classified as Crianza, Reserva or Gran Reserva by time in oak |
| Ribera del Duero | Called Tinto Fino. A darker, more powerful style |
| Douro (Portugal) | Tinta Roriz. Blended into Port and dry reds |

## With food

Its oak and aged notes go well with grilled meat, lamb chops and jamón. It's also good with sweetly marinated Korean meats like bulgogi or tteokgalbi (grilled short-rib patties).
