## Meet the grape

Pinot Noir has **thin skins** and tight bunches, making it prone to disease and sensitive to both heat and cold. It's fussy to grow, but in a cool place where it thrives, it shows a delicacy no other grape can imitate.

## How it tastes

- **Colour**: thin skins give a **pale ruby**. You can see through the glass.
- **Aromas**: red fruit like strawberry, raspberry and cherry. With age, mushroom, wet leaves and forest floor.
- **Structure**: low tannin, high acidity, light body.

## Where it grows

| Region | Style |
|---|---|
| Burgundy | Elegant and earthy. Each vineyard has its own character |
| Champagne | A key grape providing Champagne's structure |
| Central Otago (New Zealand) | Rich fruit, vibrancy |
| Oregon (USA) | Between Burgundy and California |

## With food

Light body and high acidity make it versatile with food. It's good with duck, salmon, mushroom dishes, and Korean bulgogi or japchae (stir-fried glass noodles).
